# Changing radius

Tags: BALANCE

Similar to an hourglass gate drill but freeskiing. Start with a wide radius turn and try to control the turn shape by bringing shortening the turn radius slightly on every turn. Once on a very short radius, start increasing the turn radius back to the original turn shape. 

This drill can be in reverse, starting small and increasing.

It can also be combined with other drills not involving turn shape.
