# Swiss Drill

Notes: Swiss pass, pass poles behind hips

Helps keep hips forward, instead of up, during transition and initiation. Good for those who tend to hop. Keeping the hips forward will pressure on the shovel of the ski and helps engage edge early, from the tip of the ski.
